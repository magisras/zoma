   finding 0 at rate 1.0.
2. **At half the crowd rate polite driving wins again.** Rate 0.5: careful **+Tk 168**, Dhaka
   **+Tk 86**; the batch's own verdict line: "polite driving still wins". The acceptance asks for
   both rates. (Fewer people waiting means less to race for; the racer pays more for less.)
3. **The absolute trap is gone: a careful crew eats well.** Careful nets +361 a day against food of
   Tk 30 a day (`FoodTkPerDay` 300 × `MoneyScale` 0.1): it saves about Tk 330 a day without ever
   racing, and +168 even at half the crowd. The premise is "you won't earn enough to eat", and on
   this street the safe crew does. Cause, from the numbers: **fares are real taka, the deposit is a
   tenth of a real one.** A careful 15-minute day takes Tk 791 of real fares against a zoma of
   Tk 300 (3,000 × 0.1): fares are 2.6× the deposit. On the real street the deposit (Tk 3,000–5,000,
   Ajker Patrika; see `docs/ROUTE_AND_TRIPS.md`) is about the whole of a slow day's gross (about
   Tk 3,000, RESEARCH.md), so a careful day nets near nothing or less. `docs/PLAYTEST.md` already
   noted "the pressure of the zoma is softer than in life"; since the market was tuned up in
   2fdc610 (crowds 3 a minute, cap 25) it is not soft, it is absent.

   **Expected** (owner's premise, as a check a tester can run): at both crowd rates the careful
   crew's mean net is **at or below a day's food** (it cannot save), and the Dhaka crew's mean net
   is above it (racing is how you eat). Whether to get there by scaling fares with the day, raising
   the scaled deposit, or adding the costs a real day has (the 8–10 line payments per trip,
   RESEARCH.md) is the builder's call.
4. **A "trip" barely exists in a sandbox day.** A real Mirpur 12–Azimpur trip is about 15 km,
   1.5–2.5 h at 6–10 km/h, and a day is 3–4 round trips (`docs/ROUTE_AND_TRIPS.md`). The sandbox
   ring is about 1.6 km and a day covers 1.4–2.2 km, so `trips` is 0 or 1 every day in the batch.
   The speeds are right; the day is compressed 56× in time and so is the distance. The cost: the
   rhythm the research describes (race to the end, turn, pay the line, race back, three or four
   times) never forms, and the per-trip payments (lineman, the 8–10 extortion points) barely show
   on the ledger, which is part of finding 3. Design question for the owner and builder, options in
   `docs/ROUTE_AND_TRIPS.md`.
