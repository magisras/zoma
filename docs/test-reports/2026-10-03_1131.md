## How it works today (main at the commit above)

- A rider can only get off within `ZoneHalfLengthMetres` (20 m) of their destination zone, door
  open, under `JumpSpeedMs`. Stopping anywhere else does nothing.
- The only cue is the passenger line "Stop! Stop! I'm getting off here!" (`VoiceLines` id
  `missed`), said **after** the zone is passed; the rider then rides on to the next zone
  (`Boarding.NoteMissedAlights`). No cost beyond the count.
- A rider getting off is not drawn: the passenger count just drops.

## Findings

0. **Owner's decision: getting off works the way the street does.**
   1. **The call comes before the stop.** A rider calls it 100–150 m (placeholder) before their
      place ("ওস্তাদ, সামনে নামবো", "master, I'm getting off ahead"); the helper relays it with a
      slap on the body and his own call. Sound and subtitle only: nothing on screen marks where.
   2. **Stop wherever you are.** Their place is a stretch of road, not a point at the kerb. Slow to
      a crawl or stop, in any lane; the existing rolling-door rules (speed, falls, the helper's
      pull, left foot first) apply there. No need to reach the kerb.
   3. **The rider is seen getting off.** A small box steps out of the door and walks to the nearest
      edge (kerb, or the divider if that is nearer), crossing whatever lanes lie between, as a
      pedestrian among moving traffic. Where you stopped decides how dangerous that walk is; next
      to another bus it is the gap where people are crushed (`docs/ROLLING_DOOR.md`, "Where the
      bus stops").
   4. **If you don't stop.** Riders argue, pay short, or jump off at speed (existing fall/injury
      rules); one carried past walks back angry. The ledger and the crew's lines carry it, no UI.

   **Acceptance** (a tester will check): in the sandbox, a rider's call is heard before their
   place; stopping mid-road there lets them off and their box walks to the nearest edge; driving
   on produces the argument or the jump; nothing on the HUD names the stop. The headless runner
   still finishes a day, and the thesis batch is rerun (this changes how much stopping costs, so it
   bears on finding 0 of `2026-10-03_0817.md`).
