## Facts (cite as `S-023.n`)

- **S-023.1** BIP (Bangladesh Institute of Planners) study: "The frequency of bus service (headway) is not good … no timetable is followed by the bus operators." (bip.org.bd PDF, 2013.)
- **S-023.2** Prothom Alo ("ঢাকার সড়কে বাস চালক ও ব্যবস্থাপনা কোনোটাই ঠিক নেই"): 7,937 buses, 246 routes, about 3,000 owners; অপরিকল্পিত ও অপ্রয়োজনীয় পথ (রুট) এবং অসংখ্য মালিক থাকার কারণে একই পথের বাসগুলো পাল্লাপাল্লিতে নামছে — "unplanned and unneeded routes and countless owners put same-route buses into racing."
- **S-023.3** Dhaka Tribune (292091): "The waybill monitors when a vehicle leaves a stoppage, how many passengers there are … A bus route is divided into several points and each has an inspector who notes passengers … Passengers must pay fares up to the next checkpoint." Confirmed by S-007, S-011.
- **S-023.4** After the waybill: e-ticketing from 13 Nov 2022 with "10 checkers appointed by the association" (S-003); en.prothomalo: "later POS machines became another form of extortion"; protidinerbangladesh: ওয়েবিলের নামে এখনও চলছে বাড়তি ভাড়া আদায় — "extra fares are still collected in the name of the waybill"; New Nation: "waybill and checker continues".
- **S-023.5** Prothom Alo (sig1g8jlvk, S-005): the owners' association takes বাসপ্রতি ৩৬০ টাকা থেকে ৯৬০ টাকা a day per bus (snippet; the full page gives Tk 70 openly plus GP; treat the 360–960 as the snippet's figure).
- **S-023.6** Company fines for early or late arrival, or for "cutting the time" of the bus ahead: not found anywhere; the phrase টাইম কাটা has no press hits.
- **S-023.7** Incidents of two buses of one company racing: Akash Paribahan ×2, Pragati Sarani, 9 Oct 2024, Irene killed (S-009); Shikor Paribahan ×2, Jatrabari to Mirpur, বেপরোয়া প্রতিযোগিতা ও একাধিকবার ধাক্কাধাক্কি, "reckless competition and repeated ramming" (thedailycampus.com/crime-and-discipline/259047); Moumita at Chankharpul: একই পরিবহনের আরেকটি বাস পাল্লা দিতে গিয়ে দ্রুতগতিতে এগিয়ে যায়, "another bus of the same company sped ahead racing it" (prothomalo capital/6tbre9yux0). No source describes same-company crews coordinating.
- **S-023.8** TBS Bangla calls it the driver's যাত্রীক্ষুধা, "passenger hunger" (tbsnews.net/bangla/ফিচার/news-details-168010).
- **S-023.9** Vocabulary: ওয়েবিল waybill, the segment log; চেকার the owner's inspector at a point; জিপি/গেটপাস gate pass, now a daily fee; লাইনের কাগজ line paper, the daily line fee; নাম্বার number, a rival bus of the same route; সিটিং সার্ভিস sitting service, nominally no standing and limited stops, in practice "চিটিং" (87 % charge extra, Passenger Welfare Association); লোকাল local, stops anywhere; গেটলক gatelock, doors shut after the stand, fixed stops (Gulistan long routes); কাউন্টার সার্ভিস counter service, the Feb 2025 pink buses.
