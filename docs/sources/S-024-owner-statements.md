# S-024 — The owner's statements (design truths from the build sessions, 3–5 Oct 2026)

| | |
|---|---|
| Who | the project owner, in the build chat |
| Reliability | design decisions; where they describe Dhaka, they are the owner's knowledge of the street and rank above inference |

## Facts (cite as `S-024.n`)

- **S-024.1** (4 Oct) "Buses don't wait for passengers, they drive using schedule. You cannot stop and wait. They ride in batches of 3 buses or more on the route and this is where they compete to be first on the bus station." (The owner's "schedule" means the pack's departure, not a timetable; see S-017.6.)
- **S-024.2** (3 Oct) The buses have no doors at all, or the doors are always open; people jump on and off.
- **S-024.3** (3 Oct) Rollovers are rare in real life; real buses take far steeper manoeuvres than the first sandbox allowed.
- **S-024.4** (4 Oct) People see a bus coming at 20–40 km/h and move, especially when horned; hitting a person under 10–20 km/h would not even get a fine, especially in the street rather than on the sidewalk; a 20-ton bus should push lighter cars, rickshaws and bikes.
- **S-024.5** (4 Oct) A sandbox day is charged like one real trip of six; the verdict is "careful cannot eat, Dhaka can"; build everything "as real as possible".
- **S-024.6** (4 Oct) Driving is first person from the driver's seat with wheel and mirrors, seeing what happens in the bus; the camera leaves the cab only when the body leaves the bus.
- **S-024.7** (5 Oct) "We all leave the depot with 0. We just started our shift."
- **S-024.8** (5 Oct) "Let's be reasonable: it makes no sense to calculate scrapes or fines for opposite-side driving; find a math that holds up for Dhaka." Passengers first.
