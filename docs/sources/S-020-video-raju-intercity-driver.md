## Facts (cite as `S-020.n`)

- **S-020.1** Raju, 40, drives Rajshahi–Lalmonirhat (about 250 km each way, about 500 km a day), five days a week, two days off with family; 20 years in the trade, began as an assistant.
- **S-020.2** Driver and assistant are "contract workers who earn daily wages from the bus owners"; Raju receives his daily wage from the supervisor at night: about US$ 12 for the day.
- **S-020.3** Crew of four: driver, a supervisor who checks tickets, one helper at the door guiding the driver, one for luggage (baskets of fish); counter tickets about 5 km from the depot; the bus parks overnight at a petrol station.
- **S-020.4** Speed limit 80 km/h; "Raju also sometimes drives over 100 when the road is clear."
- **S-020.5** "The hardest part comes in the afternoon when he gets sleepy and his eyelids grow heavy. Fighting that sleep is the most challenging part of his work."
- **S-020.6** Five hours out, barely an hour's break, "the schedule must be kept"; the return trip starts emptier and stops more often to fill.
- **S-020.7** Three-room house near the city with mother, wife Toma, only son and the cat Monu; highway restaurants are "a lifeline" at prices crews can afford.
