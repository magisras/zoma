## Facts (cite as `S-017.n`)

- **S-017.1** Sayedabad terminal; the guide says "more than 20,000 buses" (unverified).
- **S-017.2** Every bus has a three-man crew: a driver, a "hustler" hanging out of the door shouting the route and dragging people in, and a third who floats and collects money.
- **S-017.3** The crew lives on the bus: "all the bus staffs, they sleep at night inside the bus"; the guide says 24-hour shifts.
- **S-017.4** The driver has held a licence ten years and started driving at nine (2008); "normal in Bangladesh" (guide).
- **S-017.5** Pay: no normal wages; the team gets a cut of the takings, "maybe a tiny base pay if you're lucky"; "if the bus is empty you don't earn less, you earn nothing"; they pay the owner and the fuel, "everything contract based".
- **S-017.6** Release: "there's no spacing, there's no timetable … whenever they let them go, it's two or three buses rolling out together."
- **S-017.7** "At every stop, whoever arrives first is going to take everything. Not most of the passengers, all of them. The second bus maybe gets some scraps. The third bus gets nothing."
- **S-017.8** Tactics: tailgate inches off bumpers or bash, overtake blind, swerve to block, drive off road, brake late; "anything to get in front and stay in front of the pack."
- **S-017.9** "That bus is not going to let us pass; he swerves all over the place"; the pass succeeded by using a black car as a screen between the two buses.
- **S-017.10** "The opportunity to pass really is when you're trying to pick up a customer. Once you're on the road you've really got no shot, they'll just cut you off."
- **S-017.11** A smack into another bus at a bus stop "feels almost normal"; every bus at the terminal is dented end to end; the cockpit is bare, a speedo survives, cracks in the glass.
- **S-017.12** Framing: "a twisted Formula 1 where one team is made to fight itself"; resentment compounds between crews of the same company; "every time another bus eats while yours goes home empty, that goes in the memory bank."
- **S-017.13** Intercity rollover scene: 20–25 aboard, driver sleepy, warned twice by a passenger, crashed within five minutes on a bridge divider, bus on its side; a broken hand, nobody dead; the driver ran; locals winched the bus upright and traffic resumed; "just another Tuesday".
- **S-017.14** Night: "incredibly dangerous manoeuvres at incredibly high speeds, often at night … drivers at the end of 24-hour shifts … no runoff, just pedestrians, motorbikes, families, kids walking to school."
