# Sticks on the bus: the officer's rap, the helper's slap, the crowd

Research annex to `RESEARCH.md`, 3 Oct 2026, done in Bangla first (CLAUDE.md). Why buses in
Dhaka get hit, by whom, and what it means. Bangla quotes are given with an English translation.
Most Bangla news sites are blocked from the cloud sessions this was written in, so several points
rest on search-result snippets of the articles listed; each point says how sure it is.

## 1. The traffic officer raps the bus to move it on (confirmed, several reports)

The main answer to "why are buses beaten with sticks". Buses stop anywhere to load, often in the
middle of the road and in front of the police; the constable or sergeant hits the bus body with
the stick in his hand to say "go", without a word and without a case.

> ট্রাফিক পুলিশ সদস্যরা … হাতে থাকা লাঠি দিয়ে গাড়িতে আঘাত করে সামনে এগিয়ে যাওয়ার ইঙ্গিত দেয়।
> "Traffic police … hit the vehicle with the stick in their hand, a sign to move forward."

> জিইসি মোড়ে অবৈধভাবে দাঁড়িয়ে থাকা বাসগুলোকে সরানোর চেষ্টা
> "an attempt to move the buses standing illegally at GEC crossing" (Chattogram; a constable
> struck several buses with his stick)

Context from the same reporting: stops exist but loading happens in the road (স্টপেজ থাকলেও
যাত্রী ওঠানামা করা হয় রাস্তা থেকে, "even where there is a stop, people get on and off in the road");
the police often look away (দেখেও দেখেন না, "they see it and don't see it"). One Prothom Alo
headline quotes an officer telling a citizen who complained about a bus parked on the road:
"আপনি নাইম্যা বাস সরায়া দ্যান", "you get down and move the bus yourself".

What it is: a warning and a shove, not a fine. The bus moves twenty metres and loads again.

## 2. The helper slaps the body to talk to the driver (confirmed as practice; codes unconfirmed)

Helpers hang in the door and steer the driver by voice and by slapping the body. The code
language is well documented:

- "ওস্তাদ, বামে প্লাস্টিক" — "Master, plastic on the left": a private car, shiny and easily
  scratched, keep off it. (Daily Star Bangla, Prothom Alo column, TBS "Plastic on the left, bugs
  ahead")
- "ডানে-বামে যান ওস্তাদ" — "go right, go left, master" (Jugantor)
- "বরাবর, বরাবর" — "straight, straight", called with slaps on the body
- "ওস্তাদ, টান দিয়েন না" — "master, don't pull away": a helper begging the driver to wait
  (Prothom Alo; in that case the driver went anyway and killed an auto-rickshaw driver)
- "পেছনে নম্বর আছে" — "there's a number behind": a signal that sets the driver moving

**Unconfirmed**: one search summary gave a tap code (one slap stop, two go, three faster). A
follow-up search found no source for it. Ask a crew before building on it.

## 3. The crowd beats the bus after a hit (confirmed, very many reports)

After a bus kills or hurts someone the people nearby catch the bus, smash it with sticks and
bricks, often burn it, and beat the driver.

> পথচারীকে ধাক্কা দেওয়া বাসে আগুন বিক্ষুব্ধ জনতার — "Angry crowd sets fire to the bus that hit a
> pedestrian" (NTV)

> রাস্তা পারাপারের সময় বাসচাপায় প্রাণ গেল ছোট্ট ইয়াসিনের, বাস ভাঙচুর-অগ্নিসংযোগ — "Little Yasin
> killed by a bus while crossing; bus vandalised and set on fire" (Samakal)

The word for the beating is গণপিটুনি ("mob beating"). English sources add the 2018 Airport Road
case: two buses racing for passengers killed two students and over 100 vehicles were vandalised.
Some crews carry sticks and rods themselves (Daily Star, English).

## What the simulation could do with it (for the builder)

- **The rap.** An officer who walks to a bus loading at the junction mouth or nosing into the box
  and raps it: a sound and a shake of the camera, the crew swears, the helper calls "চলেন ওস্তাদ".
  No case, no money: the cost is the crowd you leave behind. Fits "don't explain, force it".
- **The helper's slap** as the sound of the door key (E) and of the helper's calls, so the player
  hears the stop/go before reading any subtitle.
- **The crowd after a hit.** Today a person hit ends the day. The crowd smashing the bus would add
  damage that carries to the next days (dents, glass, the bus off the road), on top of the case.
