# How the street is actually controlled

Research annex to `RESEARCH.md`, 2 Oct 2026. Signals, police, checks, crossings: what happens in
Dhaka today, with sources, and what the simulation does about each. Headlines first.

## 1. Traffic lights: poles, mostly dark

- Dhaka has about 110 signal installations and for most of the last twenty years none of them
  worked. Since 2001 more than Tk 150 crore went into signal systems that "became decorative
  poles"; the 2010–11 World Bank system at 92 junctions (solar panels, countdown timers) is out of
  order. Traffic is run by police hand signalling. [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/national/dhaka-citys-traffic-signals-not-functional-1614219509),
  [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/automated-traffic-signal-money-thrown-down-the-drain-2939261),
  [Bangladesh Post](https://bangladeshpost.net/posts/most-traffic-signals-out-of-order-in-dhaka-87360)
- 650 major junctions, about 60 lights, many dead: "the police force must be in the intersections
  directing traffic." [New Republic](https://newrepublic.com/article/118416/what-dhaka-bangladesh-traffic-capital-world-can-teach-us)
- What changed in 2025–26: a BUET-built **semi-automatic** system on the Shikkha Bhaban–Airport
  spine. Pilot from 30 Aug 2025 at seven junctions (Jahangir Gate, Bijoy Sarani, Farmgate, SAARC
  Fountain, Bangla Motor, Hotel InterContinental, the Chief Adviser's office), 22 planned from the
  High Court to the Airport. **Dual mode**: in manual mode the officer switches the lights from a
  box instead of waving; in automatic mode preset timers for peak and off-peak. Officers still
  stand in front of vehicles because "most drivers still don't follow the signals."
  [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/traffic-lights-back-dhaka-roads-order-improves-old-habits-die-hard-1275016),
  [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/dhaka-get-semi-auto-signals-tackle-jam-3851881),
  [New Age](https://www.newagebd.net/post/country/274387/pilot-signalling-system-at-seven-intersections-in-dhaka-from-aug-30),
  [BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/others/307131), [Daily Star, 2026](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/chaos-compliance-4189261)
- By mid-2026 compliance at those central junctions is visibly better: cars, bikes and CNGs stop
  at the line when the light is red and leave the zebra clear. Buses are the slowest to change.
  [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/chaos-compliance-4189261)
- **None of this is on the Mirpur 12–Mirpur 10 stretch.** The pilots and the cameras are on the
  Gulshan–Mohakhali–Farmgate–Shahbagh spine. Our corridor has dark poles and officers.

## 2. The officer at the junction: hand, whistle, body, rope

- Division of labour: **constables** are posted at junctions and control traffic; **sergeants**
  patrol, file cases and manage incident scenes. [ResearchGate (Role of Traffic Police)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/374564416_Role_of_Traffic_Police_in_Managing_Traffic_in_Dhaka_City_Challenges_and_The_Ways_Forward)
- The constable releases one direction at a time with hand signals and a whistle. Drivers don't
  stop for the hand alone, so officers **stand in front of the vehicles**; "drivers often won't
  stop unless someone is physically present in front of them." [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/traffic-lights-back-dhaka-roads-order-improves-old-habits-die-hard-1275016)
- To stop a flow they use **a rope stretched across the road**, cones, bamboo fences; at
  Gulistan's Sergeant Ahad Police Box an officer "managed countless cars, buses, and rickshaws
  using a piece of rope." [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/dhaka-city-getting-semi-automatic-traffic-signals-3973371),
  [Daily Sun](https://www.daily-sun.com/post/789178)
- In August 2024, with the police gone, students did the same job with bamboo sticks and a
  cricket bat: wave one direction through, then the other. The method is the hand, whatever holds
  it. [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2024/8/6/the-day-after-in-dhaka)

## 3. Being stopped: the sergeant, the case, the seizure

- Sergeants stop vehicles on patrol and at police boxes near hotspots (Farmgate, Shahbagh,
  Bangla Motor, Karwan Bazar, Press Club are named). Buses are the main target: obstruction,
  licence, route permit, fitness. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/166261/dhaka-traffic-unruly-despite-fines-and-cases),
  [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/372736/dhaka-traffic-rule-violations-aggravating-dhaka%E2%80%99s)
- **Targets.** DMP measures sergeants by cases filed; sergeants compete, feel pressure "to up the
  numbers", and "look for the smallest of excuses." Transport owners: "more cases are filed
  against bus companies than others." [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/73083/traffic-sergeants-compete-to-hand-out-cases)
- **Volume.** 1,400–2,300 cases a day across the city on drive days through 2025, with vehicles
  impounded and towed; a single day's fines Tk 35 lakh. [BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/others/346086),
  [BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/others/299590), [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/dmp-imposes-fines-worth-tk3514-lakh-traffic-rules-violation-single-day-961461)
- **Mechanics of a case.** The sergeant writes it on a POS machine (e-prosecution) or a paper
  slip; fines can be paid on the spot by card or bKash. The police may keep the driving licence
  until the fine is paid. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/184084/payment-of-traffic-prosecution-fine-via-debit),
  [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/backpage/news/traffic-fines-can-be-paid-spot-now-1781791),
  [RhinoCarHire guide](https://www.rhinocarhire.com/Drive-Smart-Blog/Drive-Smart-Bangladesh.aspx)
- **Amounts (Road Transport Act 2018).** Ignoring a signal or sign Tk 10,000; wrong side up to
  Tk 10,000; no licence Tk 25,000 or six months; unfit vehicle Tk 25,000 or six months (s. 25).
  Field practice is far below the book: RESEARCH.md's Tk 3,000 wrong-side case and the Tk 2,000
  camera fine are what drivers report paying. [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/penalties-violating-rules-under-new-road-transport-act),
  [Bangla Tribune](https://en.banglatribune.com/national/news/9555/Key-features-of-Road-Transport-Act-2018)
- **Seizure and dumping.** The Act lets police set up dumping yards for seized vehicles. Special
  drives against unfit and over-age buses (20 years for buses) send them to the yard; workers have
  blocked Mohakhali for hours protesting buses "held for months", demanding a 10-day maximum.
  Seized vehicles pile up in front of police stations. [BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/others/384813),
  [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/transport/transport-workers-block-mohakhali-protesting-dumping-unfit-buses-1539181),
  [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/seized-vehicles-front-dhaka-police-stations-recipe-gridlocks-579826)
- Mobile courts (an executive magistrate with police) run drives at terminals: Sayedabad, June
  2026, fines and an abandoned bus removed. [BSS](https://www.bssnews.net/others/400970)
- Police are not supposed to drive a seized bus; it is towed. DMP lacks heavy wreckers, so a
  seized or overturned bus can sit for hours. [TBS](https://www.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/transport/dhaka-traffic-worsens-after-accidents-lack-heavy-duty-wreckers-468290)

## 4. Cameras: the thing that is actually changing behaviour

- DMP's AI prosecution went live 7 May 2026: cameras read the plate, record a few seconds of
  video, match the BRTA database and SMS a case to the **owner**. Eight sites by June: Hotel
  InterContinental, Bangla Motor, Kakrail, Karwan Bazar, Bijoy Sarani, Jahangir Gate, Rampura, and
  the Gabtoli–Airport corridor. Five offences: signal jumping, wrong way, lane violation, illegal
  parking, entering a closed left lane. 120 cameras installed, 20-odd live; ~1,000 cases in the
  first weeks, a target of fully automated prosecution within six months, which would retire the
  sergeant's POS machine. [Dhaka Tribune](https://www.dhakatribune.com/bangladesh/dhaka/409621/ai-based-traffic-case-system-goes-live-in-dhaka),
  [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/news/bangladesh/news/the-cameras-are-watching-dhakas-drivers-and-bikers-are-finally-following-rules-4175756),
  [Global Voices](https://globalvoices.org/2026/06/03/ai-cameras-are-surveilling-the-roads-in-dhaka-bangladesh/),
  [Financial Express](https://today.thefinancialexpress.com.bd/last-page/dmp-to-shift-to-fully-automated-traffic-fines-in-6-months-1778520967?amp=true)
- Buses "rule Dhaka roads beyond AI watch": where there is no camera, nothing changed; Karwan
  Bazar and Farmgate are covered, most of the city is not. [Daily New Nation](https://dailynewnation.com/news/842227)
- The camera fines the owner. Who pays in the end is the open question RESEARCH.md already lists
  (repairs after a crash: owner, crew, or deducted from future zoma). The game assumes the owner
  passes it to the crew, and says so.

## 5. Crossings: the road is the crossing

- Pedestrians cross wherever they like; at peak hours "smooth traffic management becomes almost
  impossible." Foot overbridges are climbed by few; zebra crossings are not respected by drivers
  ("why this basic rule is not followed in Dhaka is riddling"). About 70 % say they prefer the
  overbridge for safety but it takes too long, so they go underneath. [Financial Express](https://thefinancialexpress.com.bd/views/why-jaywalking-1566577244),
  [Daily Star](https://www.thedailystar.net/star-weekend/city/news/why-did-the-chicken-cross-the-road-instead-using-the-footbridge-1633738),
  [ResearchGate (Progoti Sarani study)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/344558095_Pedestrian_Road_Crossing_Behavior_at_Foot_Over_Bridge_and_Zebra_Crossing_at_Progoti_Sarani_in_Dhaka_City)
- At the central junctions with the new signals, cars now leave the zebra clear on red. Away from
  them, the hand goes up and the person steps out (RESEARCH.md, Driving culture).
- Mirpur 10 is a roundabout under a metro station, a bus-loading hotspot with hawkers on the
  pavements (DNCC eviction drives there turned violent on 30 Sep 2026). People cross the ring at
  road level between the buses. [Prothom Alo](https://en.prothomalo.com/bangladesh/city/e2c4ksb6at)

## 6. Buses and the police, day to day

- Buses stop mid-road at junctions and form queues in the road at Farmgate and Karwan Bazar; the
  police mostly let it happen, and it continues outside camera coverage. [Daily New Nation](https://dailynewnation.com/news/855003)
- The lineman, the sergeant and the party man (RESEARCH.md, Bribes) are three different
  transactions: the lineman is the company's own man at the stand, the sergeant's stop is a case
  or a settlement, the party man is the local strongman's stand fee.

## 7. What the simulation does about it (changed 2 Oct 2026)

| Real thing | In the core |
|---|---|
| Dark signal poles; officer runs the junction | `Junction.Signal`: `Dark` (Mirpur, default), `Manual` (the light follows the officer's cane), `Timer` (automatic, officer backs it up). Drawn as a pole with lamps. Behaviour never changes with the lamp; only the cane and the box matter. |
| The rope | When a direction closes, the constable may rope it (`RopeChance`). A roped line lets no leakers through and holds the player's bus at the line: the world enforces. |
| Sergeants at police boxes, targets, drive days | `Checkpoint` points on the road (police boxes near junctions and stands) with a sergeant on duty by chance; on a **drive day** (random, ~1 in 5) stop chances are multiplied and the sergeant is harder to talk round. The sergeant no longer appears "after a junction" in general. |
| Seizure and the dumping yard | Refusing with bad papers risks the bus going to the yard: the day ends and the bus is gone for days (the household rests without income). |
| Cameras arriving on the route | `CameraFromDay`: from that day the first junction has a camera; a cane run or wrong side there is an SMS case to the owner, booked against the crew at the end of the day. Default: day 6, so the player meets the road without cameras first (RESEARCH story hook). |
| The road is the crossing | Pedestrians cluster near junctions and stands (60 % spawn within 40 m of one). Zebra and overbridge are scenery. |

Still not modelled, by choice for now: the mobile court at the terminal, the licence being held
until payment, towing delays, the semi-automatic central junctions (not on our corridor).
